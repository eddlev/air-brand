# AIR by VM4AI — Brand Context Pack v2

Paste this into any AI tool — ChatGPT, Claude, an image model, or AIR itself — before generating AIR brand assets, social posts, documents, decks, diagrams, or images.

## Identity

- **Name:** AIR — stands for **AI Resource**, a direct analogy to HR (Human Resources). Write the wordmark `AIR` in all caps with no period; introduce the meaning once, then use AIR.
- **Concept:** VM4AI — the concept AIR is built on. Do not describe VM4AI as a separately incorporated company unless that legal status changes.
- **Brand promise:** **AI work, carried forward.**
- **Signature:** **Focused. Fluid. AIR.**
- **Internal creative doctrine:** **The container can change. The work keeps its state.**
- **One-liner:** AIR is a prompt-based framework for structured AI work. It centers one active task and uses structured handoff state to continue work across sessions and compatible platforms without reconstructing the project.
- **Lead with** `framework` in public language. Describe what AIR does conceptually as a `governing layer`; describe how it operates in practice as a `runtime`. Never assume the reader knows `runtime`.

## Positioning

### Focused
**One active task at a time.**

AIR keeps one active task at the execution center while preserving the wider project state needed to perform it correctly. Focused does not mean narrow.

Creative state: **solid** — defined geometry, stronger alignment, denser composition, stable boundaries, one clear ember focal point.

### Fluid
**Continue, don't reconstruct.**

AIR uses structured handoff state so work can continue in another session or compatible platform without manually rebuilding the project or teaching the new session how to proceed.

Fluid describes continuity of project state. It does not claim native model memory, automatic synchronization, or uninterrupted autonomous execution.

Creative state: **liquid** — continuity between containers, connecting paths, changing placement without loss of identity.

### AIR
**The project is not confined to one provider.**

AIR is designed to travel with the work rather than requiring the work to live inside a single multi-model workspace. Compatibility depends on the capabilities and constraints of the host platform.

Creative state: **gas** — open composition, negative space, multiple possible external containers, AIR structure coherent between them.

> **Internal design note:** solid / liquid / gas are creative scaffolding for the brand system. Do not put these phase terms on standard public-facing assets unless the material is explicitly explaining the brand design system.

## Messaging hierarchy

Default order:
1. Outcome — **AI work, carried forward.**
2. Signature — **Focused. Fluid. AIR.**
3. Pain — continuing should not require rebuilding project state.
4. Value — one active task; continuation across sessions; provider independence.
5. Mechanism — structured project state, handoff, task binding, evidence, approvals.
6. Trust boundary — prompt-based, host-model governed, no guaranteed correctness or universal compatibility.

Approved supporting lines:
- One active task at a time.
- Continue, don't reconstruct.
- Your project isn't tied to one provider.
- Pick up the project. Not the briefing.
- The next session shouldn't be a restart.

`Configure. Organize. Execute.` may be used as a deeper runtime/process description, but it is no longer the primary tagline.

## Light and dark modes

Light and dark modes are equal brand expressions.

- Accents remain constant; neutrals invert.
- State is communicated through geometry, spacing, density, hierarchy, and motion — never through color alone.
- Do not rely on glow for meaning; every dark-mode visual must retain hierarchy in light mode without glow.
- On Paper, use dark ink for primary text and critical labels. Brass and Ember remain structural/focal accents rather than default small-body-text colors.
- Every visual must be tested in both themes before release.

## Type

- **Display & body:** Space Grotesk (400 / 500 / 700)
- **Mono & technical:** JetBrains Mono (400)

Do not add a new typeface to simulate fluidity or airiness.

## Mark

- Dot-in-frame: brass rounded-square frame containing one ember dot.
- The **frame is AIR structure / framework**.
- The **dot is the active task / live point**.
- A session, platform, or provider is an external container around AIR; do not redefine the canonical frame as the provider.
- The mark remains unchanged across Focused, Fluid, and AIR states.
- Do not recolor, fill, stretch, rotate, morph, or add effects.
- Do not turn the mark into a droplet, cloud, molecule, vapor, or phase icon.

## Graphic language

**One mark. Three states. One identity.**

The detailed three-state visualization is a fixed master component. Scale the complete component uniformly; never stretch, compress, or independently distort its three state panels.

### Focused
Show a horizontal task sequence. The active task is centered, larger, and fully emphasized. Neighboring inactive tasks recede with lower opacity. Supporting line: **Focus on one task.**

Opacity means inactive/non-current task in this state.

### Fluid
Show the **Current Session** as the primary full-emphasis container. **New Session** and **New Platform** are equal alternative continuation destinations and should share the same secondary styling: reduced emphasis and/or dashed outer container. They are not a chronological left-to-right sequence.

Use simple branch/continuation connectors from Current Session toward the alternatives. Do not use decorative squiggles. Supporting line: **same project state continues**.

Opacity means secondary continuation option in this state, not past/future time.

### AIR
Zoom out from task/session level to the AIR project level. Repeated AIR project marks represent the same project persisting across work containers. Show progression left to right with increasing opacity: earlier project state most transparent, intermediate state less transparent, current/latest state fully emphasized. Use simple directional arrows.

Supporting line: **same AIR project persists**. Public subline: **Stable across sessions and platforms.**

Opacity means temporal/project progression in this state.

### Background and readability

- The subtle square-outline grid is the default **expressive AIR background** for heroes, banners, social assets, diagrams, and major brand callouts.
- Plain Foundation/Paper remains the default for dense reading, documentation, and utility surfaces.
- Public-facing assets should not carry the words solid / liquid / gas as explanatory captions.
- Internal labels in explanatory diagrams must remain readable at ordinary viewing size. If they require browser or image zoom to read, increase module scale, type size, and/or contrast.
- Use the canonical AIR mark geometry and true geometric centering.
- Decorative particles, droplets, waves, clouds, vapor, smoke, and stock-AI visual clichés are off-brand.

## Motion

Motion represents state transition, never decoration.

- Focused: settle the active task into the center while inactive neighbors recede.
- Fluid: carry project state from Current Session toward either New Session or New Platform using clean, readable connectors.
- AIR: show the project persisting over time/containers, with earlier states receding and the current state resolving to full emphasis.
- Keep ordinary interaction motion short and purposeful, generally `0.2–0.4s`.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Every animated concept must have a static equivalent with the same meaning.
- Never use animation to imply automatic synchronization or autonomous continuation that AIR does not perform.

## Honesty and trust

AIR is prompt-based and host-model governed. It structures work but does not create deterministic backend enforcement.

Use the disclaimer once where a reader is deciding whether to trust output — not as a refrain.

Do not claim guaranteed correctness, universal compatibility, eliminated host drift, deterministic runtime enforcement, or compliance/certification/safety without evidence.
